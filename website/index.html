About us

Good tack is built to last for decades. We make sure it keeps working for the next rider.

Debi's Tack Boutique is a family-owned shop in Salem, Oregon. Since 2023 we've helped riders buy and sell quality used tack, kept gear in top shape with hands-on cleaning, and matched people with the right horse.

HonestWe describe condition the way it is, flaws included, for tack and for horses.
Hands-onEvery saddle and bridle is cleaned and inspected by us, never sent out.
Horse-firstA good fit for horse and rider matters more to us than a quick sale.
